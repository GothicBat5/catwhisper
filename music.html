My Music

I listen to wide ranges of music genre

K-POP

Kpop is a vibrant music genre born in South Korea that blends catchy melodies, sharp choreography, and striking visuals. It often mixes elements of pop, hip-hop, R&B, and electronic music, creating a polished sound that appeals worldwide. Beyond the music, K-Pop is known for its dedicated fan culture and the artistry of its performers.

Wonhee from Illit
LOFI

Low Fidelity or Lofi is a genre that leans into a mellow, imperfect sound. Instead of chasing studio polish, it highlights warm tones, soft crackles, and laid-back beats. In the style popularized by artists like Joji, Lofi often carries a moody, introspective edge music that feels like a night ride through the city, blending hazy melodies with subtle hip-hop or R&B influences. It’s the soundtrack for late hours, quiet thoughts, and drifting emotions.

Shot of Joji
OPM

OPM (Original Pinoy Music) refers to music created by Filipino artists, spanning a wide range of styles from heartfelt ballads to modern pop and rock. It carries the distinct voice of Filipino culture, often weaving in themes of love, resilience, and everyday life. OPM has become a proud symbol of national identity, resonating both locally and with Filipinos abroad.

Image of OPM Icon Freddie Aguilar
Tropical House

Tropical House is a subgenre of electronic dance music that radiates a breezy, summer vibe. Characterized by smooth beats, gentle synths, and tropical-inspired sounds like steel drums or pan flutes, it evokes the feeling of being on a beach getaway. The mood is light, uplifting, and easygoing music designed to transport listeners to a carefree paradise.
